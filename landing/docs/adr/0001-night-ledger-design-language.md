# Adopt Night Ledger as the landing site's design language, replacing Paper & Violet

Four alternative visual directions for the marketing site were explored in the "Keep Track design language" Claude Design project (`Design Language Directions.dc.html`): Paper & Violet (the prior, fully-documented system), The Ledger (editorial/serif), Night Ledger (dark, JetBrains Mono, mint accent), and Soft Deposit (app-native, indigo). Night Ledger was chosen and now applies to every route, including ones without an existing mockup — those are restyled by extrapolating the same token system (colors, type, nav, card patterns) rather than left in Paper & Violet.

One consequence worth flagging: the `Home.dc.html` mockup drops the screenshot gallery, the AI/Plus features section, and the pricing cards that the homepage previously had under Paper & Violet. Per explicit direction, the rework matches the provided design as given — so those sections are removed from the homepage rather than restyled forward. The standalone `/pricing` page and the Lemon Squeezy checkout routes (`/subscription`, `/success`, `/cancel`) remain in place and functional, just restyled to Night Ledger; they're no longer surfaced in the primary nav, matching the mockup's nav (Overview / Changelog / Download / GitHub).
